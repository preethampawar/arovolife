## F55 — fixed (client decision 2026-09-11: members pay the distributor price)

- **Files:** `app/app/Modules/Catalog/Models/ProductVariant.php`,
  `app/app/Modules/Commerce/Services/CartService.php`,
  `app/app/Modules/Commerce/Http/Controllers/Storefront/CartController.php`,
  `app/resources/views/shop/product.blade.php`,
  `app/tests/Modules/Commerce/DistributorPricingTest.php` (new),
  `app/tests/Modules/Commerce/StorefrontCatalogTest.php` (label expectation),
  `app/phpstan-baseline.neon`
- **Tests:** `DistributorPricingTest` F55-01 … F55-06 (6 passed); `tests/Modules/Commerce`
  196 passed; `tests/Feature/Commerce` + `tests/Modules/Returns` + `tests/Modules/Payments`
  166 passed
- **Commit:** `c0cab20c`
- **What changed:** `ProductVariant::priceForTierPaise(bool $isDistributor)` owns the tier
  rule (distributor price only where the catalogue sets one below the sale price).
  `CartService::addItem()` prices new lines at the buyer's tier, and
  `CartService::repriceForBuyer()` — called from `currentCart()`, the funnel every cart,
  checkout and shop page goes through — moves an existing cart onto the buyer's tier.
  That closes the ordinary path: a guest builds the cart, signs in at checkout. The swap
  is deliberately narrow — a line is only ever moved *between the two catalogue tiers*, so
  a line quoted at a price that has since changed keeps what the buyer was quoted.
  Everything downstream (cart totals, coupons, order items, GST, invoice) already derives
  from `cart_items.unit_price_paise`, so no other code changed; BV is untouched, being a
  property of the SKU. The PDP block now reads "Your distributor price … charged at
  checkout".
- **Baseline note:** two `phpstan-baseline.neon` entries were removed as obsolete (one made
  obsolete by the F34 `galleryImages()` generic, one by CartService no longer reading
  `sale_price_paise` directly) and two counts on `ProductVariant.php` bumped for the new
  method. Nothing else in the baseline was touched.
