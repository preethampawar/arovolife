# B11 — MSB deduction, company Arete Centre, recompute-gate banner

## F33 — fixed

The client confirmed (2026-09-10) that the Mentorship Bonus is the **fifth**
repurchase-deduction source, alongside GSB, Rank, Growth Booster and Fortune —
as the 2026-06-26 clarifications always had it ("repurchase wallet = 10% of last
month's GSB + MB + GBB + Rank + Fortune").

**Engine.** `MentorshipBonusService::creditAccrual()` now credits through
`WalletService::creditWithRepurchaseDeduction()` instead of `credit()`, so an
MSB credit writes the same three ledger entries as every other bonus
(`mb_credit` gross, `repurchase_transfer` debit, `repurchase_deduction` credit),
all stamped with the cut-off day and the earned month. `WalletService` itself was
not touched. The rate, the ₹10,000 monthly ceiling and the earned-month
windowing come from that one method, so MSB automatically honours the cap
already spent by the month's other bonuses.

**Frozen figures.** `mentorship_bonus_results` gained
`repurchase_deduction_paise` and `mb_net_paise` (migration backfills
`mb_net_paise = mb_gross_paise − deduction`, i.e. gross for every pre-decision
row), matching the four other result tables. Every MSB surface now reads those
stored facts rather than re-deriving anything from the ledger.

**Surfaces updated** (the "No repurchase deduction applies to MSB" note added by
B4a is gone from both reports):

- MSB Input & Output: `Income · Repurchase deduction · Credited to wallet`
  columns and day totals, CSV gains `Repurchase Deduction (Rs)` and
  `Credited to Wallet (Rs)`, note inverted.
- MSB Calculation report: "Received Income" → "Income (credited)" showing net
  with a `gross … · repurchase …` subline (the RB report's pattern), grand total
  and CSV likewise.
- Admin distributor **Mentorship Bonus** tab: the shared
  `<x-bonus-credit-head>` / `<x-bonus-credit-cells>` three-column pair.
- Distributor **My Income → Mentorship Bonus**: same three columns; the summary
  tiles now read "MB Credited This Month / Lifetime" and sum `mb_net_paise`.

**Docs.** `app/resources/help/compensation.md` (MSB section) states the
deduction and that pre-decision rows show gross as credited;
`docs/compensation/repurchase-client-examples-2026-09-07.md` §2.4 now separates
the two ideas — a **failed cycle** still never withholds Mentorship, but the
credit-time **deduction** does apply to it. ADC and Awards & Rewards remain
outside both.
