This page sets out the main refund terms of Arovolife Private Limited. Each section below is quoted from the published policy named in it: the [Order Cancellation Policy](/p/cancellation), the [Product Return, Warranty & Guarantee Policy](/p/returns) and the [Direct Seller Agreement & Terms and Conditions](/p/terms).

## 1. Refund for a cancelled order

*From the [Order Cancellation Policy](/p/cancellation).*

If you paid online for an order that is then cancelled, the amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after the cancellation is confirmed. Your bank or card issuer may take additional time to show the credit in your account.

arovolife may cancel an order in the following cases:

- the product is out of stock or no longer available
- the price shown on the website was wrong
- the payment could not be verified
- we suspect the order is fraudulent or in breach of our terms

If we cancel an order, you are refunded the full amount you paid.

You can cancel an order at any time before it is shipped.

- **From your account.** Sign in, open **My Orders**, select the order and choose **Cancel order**. This is available while the order is placed or paid and has not been shipped.
- **By contacting us.** Email support@arovolife.com or call +91 96405 61976, and quote your order ID. Our Customer Support team will cancel the order for you if it has not been shipped.

## 2. Refund for returned products

*From the [Product Return, Warranty & Guarantee Policy](/p/returns).*

The Refund policy is applicable only for products in saleable conditions, and partially used products (i.e. less than 30%). This policy is not applicable to products that have been intentionally damaged or misused.

Once we receive you item, we will inspect it and notify you that we have received your returned item. We will initiate a refund by online money transfer through NEFT/RTGS.

- a. Inspection – Within 7 days after receipt of material in warehouse.
- b. Refund – Within 7 days after inspection of material.

Returned products are inspected within 7 days of receipt at the arovolife warehouse. Once the inspection is complete, the refund is made within 7 days, to the original payment method or by online money transfer through NEFT/RTGS.

### Steps involved for return

- a. Return form can be downloaded from MY PROGRASSION under the tab resources if you need any assistance you can call or email Customer Support Centre, details are as under:

- b. Fill details and send scan copy back to arovolife mail id support@arovolife.com
- c. Customer Support Officers will compile data for the day & they will send data to logistic department.
- d. Logistic department will arrange pickup from members address through end-mile delivery partners.
- e. Logistic end-mile delivery partner will call Direct seller and fix appointment for pickup of return product.
- f. Once product received, inspection will be done and request will be sent to Finance for refund.

## 3. Cooling-off period

*From the [Direct Seller Agreement & Terms and Conditions](/p/terms), clause 4.*

arovolife Direct Seller understands that they have a cooling off period of 30 days to cancel the contract and receive full refund against the product purchased during this period.
