This page explains how an order placed on the arovolife website can be cancelled and how the refund reaches you. Once an order has been shipped, the [Product Return, Warranty & Guarantee Policy](/p/returns) applies instead. Delivery details are on the [Shipping & Delivery](/p/shipping) page.

## 1. Cancelling before dispatch

You can cancel an order at any time before it is shipped.

- **From your account.** Sign in, open **My Orders**, select the order and choose **Cancel order**. This is available while the order is placed or paid and has not been shipped.
- **By contacting us.** Email support@arovolife.com or call +91 88866 62949, and quote your order ID. Our Customer Support team will cancel the order for you if it has not been shipped.

## 2. Orders that have already shipped

An order that has been shipped cannot be cancelled. If you no longer want the products, or they arrive damaged or defective, follow the [Product Return, Warranty & Guarantee Policy](/p/returns).

## 3. Refund for a cancelled order

If you paid online for an order that is then cancelled, the amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after the cancellation is confirmed. Your bank or card issuer may take additional time to show the credit in your account.

## 4. Cancellation by arovolife

arovolife may cancel an order in the following cases:

- the product is out of stock or no longer available
- the price shown on the website was wrong
- the payment could not be verified
- we suspect the order is fraudulent or in breach of our terms

If we cancel an order, you are refunded the full amount you paid.

## 5. Effect on Business Volume (BV)

When an order is cancelled, the Business Volume (BV) recorded for that order is reversed, in the same way as for a returned order.
